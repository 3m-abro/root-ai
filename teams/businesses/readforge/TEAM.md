# ReadForge Team

## Purpose

Own the product and business outcomes of ReadForge.

## Responsibilities

- product roadmap
- author and publisher needs
- reader experience
- pricing and monetization
- acquisition strategy
- product KPIs
- prioritization

## Shared Department Usage

Examples:

- Engineering: Flutter, backend, APIs, infrastructure
- Marketing: author acquisition, publisher acquisition, growth
- Research: competitors and publishing-market research
- Operations: onboarding and support processes
- Finance: pricing and unit economics
- Legal: publishing agreements, privacy, terms and compliance

## Rule

The ReadForge Team owns WHAT and WHY.

Shared departments own specialist HOW.
