# ClearStack Studio Team

## Purpose

Own the business outcomes of ClearStack Studio.

This team decides what ClearStack Studio needs.
Shared departments decide how specialist work should be executed.

## Responsibilities

- business strategy
- service offering
- sales priorities
- client delivery priorities
- revenue goals
- roadmap
- KPI tracking
- identifying work that should be delegated to shared departments

## Shared Department Usage

Examples:

- Engineering: software development and automation
- Marketing: acquisition, SEO, campaigns and content
- Research: market and competitor research
- Operations: workflow and delivery automation
- Finance: business economics and reporting
- Legal: contracts and compliance analysis

## Rule

Do not create permanent specialist agents inside this business team
when the capability already exists in a shared department.
